# Alberta and Calgary Notes

Regional notes for users based in or targeting Alberta, especially Calgary.
**Last checked: November 2026.** Organizations, programs, pay rates and rules
change; verify anything here against the official source before relying on
it, and tell the user the date of the source you used.

## Salary sources
- **Job Bank wage reports:** search by occupation and choose the Calgary
  Region (or Edmonton, or Alberta) for local figures. Reports are organized
  by NOC code.
- **alis Alberta Wage and Salary Survey:** occupation-level wages for Alberta,
  using NOC 2021 five-digit codes. The most recent survey at the last check
  was 2023, so treat its figures as a baseline, not current market pay.
- **Public-sector employers:** the Government of Alberta, the City of Calgary
  and health agencies often show pay in postings or publish salary schedules
  and collective agreements. Use these when a public-sector posting omits a
  range.
- **Pay disclosure:** Alberta does not currently require pay ranges in job
  postings, so a missing range is common and not a warning sign by itself.
- **Minimum wage:** Alberta's general minimum wage was $15.00 per hour at the
  last check. Use it only as a floor reference, and confirm the current rate.

## Main sectors in Calgary
Calgary Economic Development lists these as key industries: aerospace,
agribusiness, defence, digital and creative, energy and environment, film and
TV, financial services, health and life sciences, technology, and
transportation and logistics.

When assessing sector fit, note that energy and utilities employers hire
for IT, analysis and project roles too, not only engineering, and often use
sector vocabulary (for example operations, safety, regulatory, field
services) that the user may need to learn.

## Employer types worth checking
- Energy, utilities and pipeline companies (large IT, data and project teams).
- Managed service providers (MSPs) serving small and mid-sized businesses.
- Public sector: Government of Alberta, City of Calgary, school boards,
  post-secondary institutions.
- Health: Alberta's public health system has been reorganized since 2024 into
  several agencies alongside Alberta Health Services (such as Recovery
  Alberta and Primary Care Alberta). Check which organization is actually
  posting the role and use its own pay information.
- Financial services, logistics and aviation employers with in-house IT.

## Practical requirements common in Alberta postings
- **Driver's licence:** Class 5 is the standard Alberta licence. Field IT and
  site-based roles often ask for one plus a vehicle. Flag it if the profile
  does not show one; do not guess how long a licence exchange takes.
- **Commute:** Calgary is spread out. Industrial and business parks can be
  hard to reach by transit, so compare the work location with the user's
  stated commute limits.
- **Background checks:** police information checks are common for health,
  education and government roles.

## Local job search support
Settlement and employment organizations in Calgary offer free resume
reviews, workshops and mentoring, often aimed at newcomers. Examples listed
by the City of Calgary include:
- Calgary Catholic Immigration Society (CCIS)
- Calgary Region Immigrant Employment Council (CRIEC)
- Centre for Newcomers
- Directions for Immigrants in Trades and Professional Careers (Bow Valley
  College)
- YWCA Calgary
- Making Changes Employment Association of Alberta (programs for women)
- Windmill Microlending (loans for credential recognition and training)

Mention these only when the user asks about support or appears to be new to
the Canadian job market, and do not describe eligibility rules; point the
user to each organization's own website.

## Coaching notes for Alberta
- Many entry-level IT and analysis roles in Calgary sit inside non-tech
  employers. Suggest reading the employer's sector, not just the job title.
- Public-sector postings usually include screening questions and structured
  competencies; match answers to the posting's wording with real evidence.
- If the user would consider Edmonton or other Alberta cities, check whether
  the same role pays differently there before comparing offers.
